Operational decision workflow

HealPipe combines fault diagnosis, reconstruction, validation-calibrated confidence, preserved data status, and an explicit accept-or-refuse decision. Logging and data cleaning are operational components, not claimed as novel contributions.

  1. Diagnose: classify missing, freeze, spike, or drift evidence.
  2. Reconstruct: estimate affected readings using interpolation or cross-sensor predictions.
  3. Validate: score repair confidence with a model calibrated on validation data.
  4. Decide: accept a supported repair or refuse it and preserve a null output as Unresolved.
  5. Audit and evaluate: retain point-level evidence and assess detection, repair accuracy, confidence reliability, and abstention together.
